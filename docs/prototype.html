How an athlete uses it

  1. Build a profile: film, measurables, stats, socials and eligibility (NCAA/NIL status, pro status).
  2. The AI turns game stats and film into a scouting report: production, best-fit play styles, strengths and weaknesses. Teams and scouts can read it directly.
  3. Get matched each week to tryouts, combines, pro leagues at home and abroad, and brands.
  4. Price every offer, run the contract check, and send a counter-offer drafted by the AI.
  5. Track deliverables, payments and taxes in one place. Bring in a lawyer for the big contracts only.
